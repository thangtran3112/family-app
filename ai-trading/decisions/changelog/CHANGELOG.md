- **A re-scan on a running clock no longer wedges the Desk (2026-11-02):** a running thread clock makes every read of its day take the day list's row lock, and FastAPI closes a request's session only after the request's background tasks, so a re-scan started on a running day waited for a lock its own request held (a deadlock Postgres cannot see). Every poll of the thread queued behind it, each holding one of the 15 pooled connections and a worker thread, until every `/api/desk/*` route timed out (Cloudflare 524, and 500s from the pool: the clock resume, the chart's advance). Preps and re-scans now run on a detached thread (`api/replay.py` `_detach`), never as a background task of the request; the API's connections carry `lock_timeout` (10 s, a plain 503 "busy · try again") and `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` (5 min) so no future stuck holder can take the pool. Regression: `tests/api/test_replay_clock_wedge.py`.
