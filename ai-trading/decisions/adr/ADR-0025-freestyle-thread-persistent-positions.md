# ADR-0025: Freestyle thread — one timeframe sandbox, positions persist across time

Status: accepted 2026-11-03, owner's directive. Supersedes [ADR-0023](ADR-0023-open-to-open-days-jump-no-end-day.md) (whole); amends [ADR-0016](ADR-0016-practice-broker-ticket.md), [ADR-0018](ADR-0018-server-thread-clock.md) (amendments (a)-(b)), [ADR-0019](ADR-0019-away-orders-fill.md) (lazy → eager booking) and [ADR-0022](ADR-0022-one-thread-time.md) (day-thread scoping and the cap clauses). [ADR-0017](ADR-0017-thread-clock.md) stands. Design: `plan-freestyle/plan.md` (2026-11-03).

**Why.** A day thread derives its position from the marks of one name in one *day*, and the day hand-over (`plan.end_day` → `session.end`) sells every open position at the tape's end and books it as realized. A forward **Jump** across an open triggered that hand-over, so a position the user forgot to exit vanished on the next day's empty list — the reported bug. Swing persisted positions but was a second model with a second clock and a second UI. The owner's directive removes the distinction entirely: *"Both Day trade and Swing Trade are now combined into freestyle trading with time tuning… when jumping existing positions must persist… A position holding until a future date will automatically showing Gain or Loss based the future price at the future jumped date-time… Think of this as a timeframe sandbox investment experience."*

**The decision.**

- **One thread type — freestyle.** No `mode`, no day/Swing split. A freestyle thread is a paper account plus one clock; you trade any name the thread tracks and move time forward.
- **Positions persist.** A position changes only by (1) your explicit Close/Sell/Buy, (2) a resting order *you placed* filling, or (3) a corporate-action restatement. The clock, a Jump, Next open, pause, logout, thread close — none of them close or flatten anything. A thread close cancels working orders and freezes `T`, leaving positions open and valued at `T`.
- **Jump and Skip day both preserve positions.** "Skip day" is renamed **Next open** — a Jump preset to the first 9:30 ET open after `T`, through the same `POST /threads/{id}/clock {"to"}`. The only things that close a position are your trade or a fill.
- **Forward only, everywhere.** A past `to` returns **422** ("Time only moves forward"), a database write guard (`clock_at <= :t`) stops two tabs rewinding, and every other time-movement affordance (session `advance(to|step)`, the chart JumpMenu, "To the next signal", standalone "Replay again") is removed.
- **Mark to the jumped time.** Equity = realized P/L plus every open position marked at `T`. A position Jaguar a month ahead shows gain/loss at that date-time. No price at `T` falls back to the prior session's last print (never carried at cost, never closed).
- **The day apparatus is replaced.** The per-day list → one **Screen** per thread (her filters re-scanned at `T`); the block of days (5–30 sessions, "Open day N"), per-day review and per-day journal gating, list-time gating ("list comes out at", `list_pending`), the practice window, "Pass/skipped day", and "days held" are deleted. The journal is read live, grouped by ET date. The ET calendar date survives as a label/key only — it never ends, resets or reviews anything.
- **Upper bound is data, not a lesson.** `horizon` = the extended close of the most recent session available under the 15-minute embargo. A run/Jump past it → 422; a running clock clamps there and pauses (`pause={kind:"horizon"}`) — the only thing that stops time on its own.
- **Capacity restated.** `MAX_ACTIVE_THREADS = 10` counts **all** threads. The 49-name cap applies to the Screen **and** to names a thread holds with a position or order.
- **Order lifetime (amends ADR-0016).** Time in force **GTC (default) | Day**. A Day order expires at the extended close of its placement session (the *market's* rule, shown on the ticket). A closing order is derived **reduce-only** (`min(order, position)` at fill; cancelled "Position already closed" if the position is gone) so a forgotten protective stop cannot open a new position days later.
- **Resting orders fill eagerly** as the clock crosses them — including across days inside a Jump — booked at settle, not lazily at next touch (amends ADR-0019).
- **Migration.** Day threads' open-day sessions become lanes (open positions carry; balance and equity invariant). Swing threads run a dry-run-first reconcile script that **converts** open holds into freestyle positions anchored at the restated basis. Standalone single-day sessions are read-only until the contract migration. `0021_freestyle_expand` (additive) now; `0022_freestyle_contract` (irreversible, backup first, owner sign-off) in a later release.

**Consequences.** A position lives through the close, the night, a weekend, a month-long Jump, until the trader closes it. Forced day-end liquidation, the "did the list win" review and the practised-vs-skipped gamification are gone — the lane reads as a neutral broker timeframe sandbox. Swing's code, tables, routes and UI are deleted (pure `holds` rules are salvaged into `replay/positions.py` first). Signal-adherence scoring, pre-market hiding and corporate-action edge-cases are kept as-is for v1 (recorded in the plan §10 D7/D8/D9).

Decisions the owner is expected to course-correct on, recorded here: D1 order-lifetime default (GTC) and reduce-only; D2 stop-as-alert-only; D3 convert-not-close swing holds; D4 delete standalone replay; D5 frozen universe; D6 delete `next_signal`; D7 keep signal scoring; D8 premarket hidden; D9 split/delisting known-imperfect; D10 open-ended thread; D11 name "Freestyle".
