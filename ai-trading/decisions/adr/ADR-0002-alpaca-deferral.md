# ADR-0002: Alpaca $99 tier deferred

Status: accepted 2026-10-31 (owner). Sources: [02b §4](../../plans/subplans/02b-desk-market-data-options.md), [02t §1](../../plans/subplans/02t-market-data-bridge-plan.md).

Decision: the **Alpaca Algo Trader Plus $99/month tier stays unpurchased** until the app is tested working with bugs fixed and feature requests done, and the wife reaches a professional level. Practice and development stay on free/delayed data (Alpaca Basic SIP, delayed 15 minutes on the free plan).

Consequences: no paid subscription in any plan; switching to the paid tier later is keys plus env only, no code change; OPRA options (requiring the signed agreement plus the tier) stay gated, and the UI must say so honestly instead of faking paid data.
