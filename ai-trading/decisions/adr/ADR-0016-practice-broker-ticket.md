# ADR-0016: Practice trades through a broker-style ticket

Status: proposed 2026-11-01, built on `feature/practice-neutral-broker`; the owner's directive, to be confirmed in review of the pull request. Amended 2026-11-01 (`feature/practice-multi-position`): "one open trade per name" is resolved, see "One position per name, many fills".

Decision: the Practice replay workspace places trades the way Webull or Interactive Brokers does, against a replayed past day. It is a simulator, not a broker: nothing reaches an account, a broker or live data.

- **The ticket.** A side (Buy or Sell), a quantity and an order type. Market needs nothing else; Limit takes a limit price; Stop a stop price; Stop-limit both. Close position sits with the open position, not in the ticket. A note is a quiet action beside the footnote.
- **No coaching.** A stop is advisable and never required: no field, no disabled button, no warning, no sizing hint, no max-loss banner. The thread plan's "stop on every entry" is still a score in the journal, and a new thread no longer starts with it on.
- **One size rule: cash.** An entry is refused only when its notional is more than the free cash (`holds.check_buying_power`: day threads against the balance less the day's open trades, swing threads against free cash). The five-trade day cap, the five-hold swing cap and the 10,000-share contract bound are gone; the share bound is now the width of the `shares` column.
- **Honest fills.** A market entry fills at the last print. A limit, stop or stop-limit entry waits in `replay_sessions.orders` (migration 0016) and fills from `advance`, only on a later one-minute bar, at a price that bar proves (`replay/orders.py` has the rules and tests). A limit through the market fills at once at the last print. A fill is stamped at its bar's close and is an ordinary entry mark, so the journal, ledger and holds read it like any other. A fill the account cannot afford is cancelled with that reason; what still waits when the day ends is cancelled.
- **Not simulated: Bracket.** The ticket names it and cannot place it. A stop and a target that cancel each other would need exits that execute on the tape, and a stop here is still an alert (play pauses, the user chooses): "stops never exit" is unchanged.

Guards changed, each narrowly. The Desk had three guards that no route, table, e2e screen or request says "order", "position" or "broker". The Practice replay workspace's two routes (`POST /replay/sessions/{id}/orders`, `DELETE .../orders/{order_id}`) are now named in `tests/api/test_mvp_routes.py`, `tests/e2e/technical_check.py`, `lib/strategy/stage.test.ts` and `e2e/desk/helpers.ts`; the screen check skips only `/practice/replay`. Every other route and screen still fails the guards. The Practice-only banned list (order, position, fill, broker, execute) is gone; the desk-wide technical list stays. Order storage is a column on `replay_sessions`, so the "no table named order or fill" check passes untouched.

One position per name, many fills (amendment; resolves the first version's deferral of scale-in, partial exit and flipping). The owner's rule: no limit on buying into or selling out of one stock, as long as it is within the total day-trade balance. A name now holds ONE **average-cost position**, and every Buy or Sell is a fill against it. Cash stays the only size rule.

- **The model.** A Buy (`long_entry`) or Sell (`short_entry`) mark is a signed fill. The side the position already has adds to it at the weighted average cost; the opposite side closes shares first (up to the position's size) and opens the other way with what is left (a flip). A fill is one mark, one request and one price: a flip is not two marks. Close position (`long_exit` / `short_exit`) still closes the whole position. Average cost is carried to six decimals (the price column's scale), halves up, and is the number the screen shows and the P/L runs on (D-033). Chosen over lots because it prices every fill correctly with no rule about which lot an exit takes, because it is what the ledger and the R basis can express with the least change, and because a first-in-first-out shortcut would price the second lot of a partial exit wrongly.
- **A trade is one realized exit.** The walk (`journal.assemble`) returns the open position and one trade per mark that closes shares, at the average cost then; a sale of part of a position leaves the cost alone. So a journal trade, a ledger row and a cent of P/L correspond one to one, and the ledger stays keyed by the realizing mark. A position closed in three parts is three trades that share a position number. The adherence scores are per trade, except the max-loss rule, which scores every fill that opened or added to a position (an add after the loss line is an entry too). The trade limit counts trades.
- **The stop belongs to the position, not to one fill.** The first stop set on a position is its R basis (a later stop moves the line, never the R); R is measured from the average cost then. A flip starts a new position and so a new stop. Scored (stop on every entry, stop honored), never required, and never exits. A stop given with an entry that only reduces moves the remaining position's line. Notes stay on the mark.
- **Buying power is applied to the shares a fill opens.** Free cash is the thread balance less the entry notional of every open position of the day or thread, this name's included (what she already holds is cash spent). The shares that close a position need no cash; a flip closes the whole position first, so its notional and the closed shares' P/L come back before the opening shares are checked. No cap on fills, lots or shares of a name.
- **Honest fills are unchanged.** A working order is a Buy or Sell like a market mark and nets against whatever the position is when it fills (`replay/orders.py` is untouched in its rules). An order is cancelled only for cash, with the reason. Nothing is filled that the matcher did not produce, and a fill is stamped at its bar's close with the clock moving forward only.
- **Swing holds.** The name's one open `practice_holds` row IS its average-cost position: a second buy adds shares and re-averages `entry_price` in place (`hold_rows.scale_in`), a sale of part closes a slice as a closed row of its own at the same cost with `part_exit = true` (`hold_rows.split_off`) while the open row keeps the rest, and a flip closes the row and opens the next. A slice is a trade for the ledger and the review's P/L and credits to the ledger when the day ends, like every swing exit; it is not a hold opened, so the day summary's `entries` and `max_open_seen` and the weekly entry facts skip it.
- **Undo.** Only the last mark can be undone, before the clock moves, as before. A day undo deletes the mark and the position is recomputed from the marks. A swing undo reverses the mark's two halves: a hold it opened is deleted, a scale-in is restored from `practice_marks.position_before` (the hold as it stood, because a weighted average cannot be un-averaged to the digit), a slice rejoins the open row, a hold it closed reopens, and either must still pass the cash rule (409 otherwise).
- **Database (migration `0017`).** Two additive columns: `practice_marks.position_before` (JSONB) and `practice_holds.part_exit` (boolean, default false). The partial unique index "one open hold per symbol in a thread" is KEPT: it is no longer a limit on how much she may hold, it is the model's invariant (one average-cost position per name) and the backstop against two racing first entries. Multi-lot is legal at the storage layer because the lots live in the marks, the audit trail, and the position row is their average. `downgrade()` drops both columns; a session that already holds scale-ins or partial exits would be misread by the previous journal, so downgrade only before the feature is used.
- **Screen.** B and S are always a fill with the ticket as it stands (Buy adds to a long or buys back a short, Sell the other way); X closes the position. The Marks list reads "Practice buy 40" and "Practice sell 40" (a Sell can be an exit, a flip or an entry, so the old "long" and "short" words would mislead); the position card reads "Avg price". No coaching copy was added, and the Bracket line and its "not simulated" wording are unchanged.

Not done, and why: the partial exit of a swing hold is a closed row, so the weekly review lists the slice beside its position as its own hold fact (realized P/L is right; the "holds" count of a week is not reduced by it). Close position still closes everything; a quantity on it is not offered. The star and focus-name gates and the plan's own scoring limits (max trades, max loss, risk per hold, all optional) are scored or navigational, not size rules, and are untouched. (`maxOpenHolds` was in this list in the first amendment and is gone: see the neutrality amendment below.) An order on a name fills only while that name's replay runs, so a name left behind the day clock is not scanned: it can under-fill, never over-fill.

Neutrality amendment (2026-11-01, `feature/practice-neutrality-fixes`; an adversarial review of the ticket found two places where the rule "cash is the only size rule" was still false):

- **Standalone sessions run the cash rule.** A session with no thread (`POST /replay/sessions`, which the journal's "Replay again" uses) used to skip the check, so any quantity was accepted. It now has an account of its own: the thread default starting balance (`DEFAULT_STARTING_BALANCE`, $50,000) plus its own closed trades, less the entry notional of its open position (`session._day_free_cash`). Every attempt starts from the same balance, nothing is booked from it, and a thread's balance is untouched. Chosen over removing the path: "Replay again" and a session opened outside a day list are supported drills, and a refusal with the same words ("Not enough practice cash · $50,000 free") is what a broker would give.
- **One rule, every path.** A day or standalone entry (market fill, working order at placement, working order at fill) calls `_check_day_buying_power`; a swing entry calls `holds.check_buying_power` with the thread's free cash. The undo of a day exit or sale, which puts shares back, now passes the same cash rule against the other names (409 "Can't undo that · it would leave you without enough practice cash"), as the swing undo already did.
- **No cap on open holds.** The swing setup no longer asks for "Open holds at most" (`maxOpenHolds`, 1 to 5, default 3): the field is gone from the contract, the setup form, the thread journal and the plan checklist, and the weekly review has no `open_holds_within_plan` check. The checklist still shows how many holds are open, as a count. A neutral broker does not ask how many positions you will keep; the plan keeps only rules the user chooses to set (stop note, signals only, max loss, max trades, risk per hold, all optional) and scores them, never blocking. A swing plan stored before this change keeps the key in its JSON; it is ignored on read, and a week with no rule set scores `null` (nothing to score) instead of 100.

Orders in flight amendment (2026-11-01, `feature/practice-fix-backend`; the review of the ticket and of the clock found the same gap twice: what buying power and "nothing she did not see" mean while an order waits):

- **A working order holds its cash back.** `_day_free_cash` and `_swing_free_cash` subtract, for every working order of the day (every name, the thread's cash is shared), the entry notional of the shares it would open or add to against its name's position as it stands, at its limit (a stop at its trigger); the shares it would close need no cash. A second order that the first leaves no cash for is refused when it is placed (and so is a market entry), instead of being cancelled when it fills. Each fill releases its own reserve (`Ctx.filling`) and a settled order stops reserving at once. The check at the fill stays as the safety net: a sell limit the tape gaps through fills above its limit, so a short's notional can outgrow the reserve and is cancelled with the cash reason.
- **No fill in a span she was on another name.** A name catching up to the day clock (an `advance`, or the lift of a resumed name in `start_session`) skips `(playhead, clock]`: it is history to her, and the journal already scores no signal in it. A working order the tape would have filled there is cancelled with "The price reached it while you were on another name · it did not fill" (`session.settle_away_orders`), so no trade is scored that she did not see, no fill is stamped before the clock, and no later touch fills the order as a different trade. A stop-limit whose stop leg only triggered there keeps working with its trigger recorded. This replaces "can under-fill, never over-fill" above with a visible outcome.
- **Whole cents.** A limit or stop price must be in whole cents (422 on `limitPrice` / `stopPrice`): a fill is rounded to the cent half up, so a 49.505 buy limit would have filled at 49.51, past its limit. The cash shortfall sentence rounds the free cash down ("$450 free" for $450.60).
- **Left as designed:** a stop-limit whose limit is on the far side of its stop (a buy stop at 51 with a limit at 49.5) stays legal: it is a pullback entry and `orders.py` documents it.
